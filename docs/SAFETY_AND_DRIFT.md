## Limits

- The vocabulary check has almost no power: one rare word added to 60% of reports is seen no more often than by chance.
- References roll, so a lasting shift stops being flagged after about a year (pacemakers, 2026Q3). Thresholds are wide because normal quarters vary a lot.
- The volume check was not blind: yearly totals were seen when sizing the download.
- The red-team cases are single-turn with short, obvious hostile text; a clean sweep of raw answers says little about disguised or multi-turn attacks.
- The pass rules do not test accuracy. R06 and R17 told the user a correct Insulet figure did not match the data, because they counted every maker.
- The guard, the labels, the cases and the verifier come from one author. The verifier is separate code, recomputes every drift statistic, grade and dollar, but recomputes only one of the two required planted shifts and cannot judge whether a label is right.
- Post hoc: 2 new hip reports with empty problem terms are read without them; ties in the word list are broken by spelling. Neither is in the baseline or changes a flag.

## Why we did these tasks

| Task | Why we did it | What we learned | What it changes next |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-registered red-team cases and drift rules | To stop rules bending to results | The failed guard could not be explained away | Same for any new guard |
| Held-out tests of each detector | A checker tuned on its own examples looks perfect | Patterns 12/12 on their own pairs, 2/15 on unseen ones | Never trust a detector on its own examples |
| A Haiku judge as guard | Patterns cannot read meaning | Good on single sentences, over-cautious on real answers | Validate on full-length answers; do not ship this guard |
| Hand labels before judging | Grading with the guard would pass by construction | Every raw answer labelled safe, by a non-independent reader | Ask an outside reader before claiming safety |
| Drift on real new reports | A tool must notice when its data changes | Found the pacemaker battery surge; found two weak checks | Fixed reference and a better vocabulary check |
| Independent verifier | Numbers must survive a second implementation | Evidence 5/5; criteria met 4/5 | Runs in CI |
